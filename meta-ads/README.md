# Yalla Movers & Maintenance — Meta Ads Launch Pack

## Campaign 1 — Home moving

- **Goal:** WhatsApp conversations
- **Audience:** Dubai residents, 24–55, interests in property rental, Dubai Marina, JVC, Business Bay, JLT and furnishing.
- **Primary text:** Moving home in Dubai? Get the right truck, careful movers and clear communication in one WhatsApp message.
- **Headline:** Your next move, without the drama.
- **CTA:** Send WhatsApp Message

## Campaign 2 — Pickup truck & furniture

- **Goal:** WhatsApp conversations
- **Audience:** Dubai residents buying or selling furniture, appliances or household items.
- **Primary text:** One sofa, a full room or a new furniture find — book a 1, 2 or 3-ton pickup with the helping hands you need.
- **Headline:** Truck + labour, ready when you are.
- **CTA:** Send WhatsApp Message

## Campaign 3 — Labour supply

- **Goal:** WhatsApp conversations
- **Audience:** Warehouse, retail, fit-out and facilities decision-makers in Dubai.
- **Primary text:** Need reliable hands for loading, unloading, stock shifting or a moving day? Tell us the crew size and job details on WhatsApp.
- **Headline:** Labour support for the real job.
- **CTA:** Send WhatsApp Message
